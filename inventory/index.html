SAMPLE DATA

Every SKU's on-hand count is checked against its PAR level and its weekly sales velocity from the POS. It covers the full alcohol program, from draft kegs to spirits, wine, and cans. Items are sorted by urgency, and anything marked Cut on the Keep / Cut tab drops off the order so you sell through it instead of restocking it.

ItemCategoryOn handPARUse / wkWeeks leftStatusOrderEst. cost

Use / wk is in stock units (kegs, bottles, or cases), converted from POS servings. PAR = weekly usage × 1.6 weeks of cover (1.4 for kegs) with weekly deliveries. Order now = under half a week left. Order soon = under one week. Overstock = more than four weeks on hand, over twice PAR, and at least two units above it.

Every alcohol SKU is scored against the others in its category on sales velocity, margin, guest rating (beer and wine), and supply stability, with a penalty when too many items overlap the same style. Wells and core sellers are always kept. Drag the weights to match what matters to you and every call updates instantly, including the reorder list.

ItemCategorySold / wkPriceCost %RatingSupplyScoreCallWhy

Sold / wk = servings (pints, pours, glasses, or cans). Cost % = unit cost ÷ sellable servings (90% keg yield, 95% bottle yield) ÷ menu price, judged against each category's own target range. Keep ≥ 60, Watch 42–59, Cut < 42.
